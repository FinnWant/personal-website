MoKnowledge

  • TypeScript
  • Next.js
  • PostgreSQL

Paste a company's website and MoKnowledge crawls it, extracts a structured knowledge base from the real HTML, and shows exactly where every value came from.

  • Never guesses: missing details are marked “Not found”
  • Conflicting answers on the site are shown side by side for a one-tap fix
  • Asks a few ranked questions to fill the most valuable gaps
  • Versioned JSON export, with an optional multi-tenant Supabase/PostgreSQL backend
